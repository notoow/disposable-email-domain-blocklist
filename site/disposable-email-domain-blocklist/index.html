Disposable Email Domain Blocklist dataset

A small, reviewable starting dataset for developers who need to stop temporary email signups without turning a free-provider list into a blunt instrument.

What is in the dataset? Each record names a domain, classification, confidence, evidence URL, evidence note, provider, first/last verification dates, subdomain behavior, and lifecycle status.

The record is supported by VerifyMail's domain report, which identifies it as disposable and attributes the provider. The project records facts and attribution; it does not copy a third-party bulk list.

Default decision policy

Blocked

Only active + disposable + medium/high confidence.

Allowed by default

Free providers, aliases, role addresses, unknown, low-confidence, stale, disputed, and removed records.

Explicit scope

A record with block_subdomains=true matches only that domain and its descendants—not its parent or sibling domains.

Contribute a domain

Submit a normalized ASCII hostname, HTTPS evidence URL, short note, dates, confidence proposal, and subdomain policy through the domain report form. False-positive and removal requests are welcome.
